1.Feature:AI-based Ayurvedic Prakriti identification — Vata, Pitta, or Kapha.

2.Facial Analysis:Extracts 20 facial features and groups them into 
Geometry, Texture, Colour, and Symmetry modules.

3.Module Fusion:Each module calculates Vata/Pitta/Kapha scores and confidence, 
then confidence-aware fusion combines the four modules into the final facial scores.

4.Questionnaire & Final Result:Combines 30% facial analysis + 70% adaptive 
questionnaire to identify the dominant Prakriti, secondary Prakriti, scores, and confidence.

5.Technologies:React.js, Vite, Tailwind CSS, Python, FastAPI, OpenCV, MediaPipe, NumPy, Scikit-learn, and Random Forest.
